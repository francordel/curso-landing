01 / syllabus

What you'll learn

  • 01

    Finding topics

    What's moving in your niche every week, without spending the evening on Twitter.

  • 02

    Script and voice

    30-second scripts that hook, and your cloned voice reading them for you.

  • 03

    Automatic editing

    Your face and working shots, the b-roll and the compositions, cut to the word.

  • 04

    Subtitles with style

    Karaoke, capsules and art-direction moments that place themselves.

  • 05

    Covers and captions

    Cover, description and hashtags for every video, in every language.

  • 06

    Publish without touching anything

    The agent schedules on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube and replies to comments.

02 / workflow

How it works

  1. 01

    You film one afternoon of shots

    Gestures, hands, screen. A bank of generic shots that works for hundreds of videos.

  2. 02

    The agent produces

    Topic, script, voice, editing, subtitles, cover and caption. You look at a contact sheet.

  3. 03

    You review and publish

    Fix whatever you want and the agent leaves it scheduled on your accounts.
